# kevinkelchen.com

Kevin Kelchen's professional personal site. Its primary content is writing. The professional bar is editorial — Kevin publishes only what stands a professional first impression — not a mechanical classification; Posts carry no professional/personal designation.

## Language

**Post**:
A single piece of writing published on the site. All Posts live in one chronological stream — there are no separate blogs, sections, or post classifications.
_Avoid_: Article, entry, note

**Tag**:
A topical label on a Post (e.g. `software-engineering`). Tags are recorded on every Post from day one but have no pages or navigation in v1.
_Avoid_: Category, taxonomy

**Homepage**:
The landing page: professional introduction first, then recent Posts.

**Archive**:
The complete reverse-chronological list of all Posts.
_Avoid_: Blog index, post list

**Feed**:
The single RSS/Atom feed, containing every Post.

**About**:
The dedicated page describing Kevin's background and work. Contact is via LinkedIn and X links only — no email address appears anywhere on the site. Location is never stated.
